## How we picked

Criteria that matter on a real site:

- **Durability**: sole that doesn't split after 4 months of concrete and rebar
- **8+ hour comfort**: footbed, arch support, cushioning
- **Safety**: ASTM-certified steel or composite toe
- **Real cost**: price per year of use, not sticker price

## Steel vs composite toe: which do you need?

| | Steel toe | Composite toe |
|---|---|---|
| Protection | Maximum | Very good |
| Weight | Heavier | Lighter |
| Cold touch | Yes (conducts) | No |
| Metal detectors | Triggers them | Passes |

Falling-object risk (structural, demo): steel, no question. If you pass through detectors (airports, some plants) or work outdoors in winter: composite.

## FAQ

**How often should I replace construction boots?**
With daily use, soles wear out in 8–14 months. If your feet already hurt or the heel is eaten, it's past time.

**Are $200+ boots worth it?**
Yes — a quality boot costs less per year than two cheap ones, and your back has no replacement price.
