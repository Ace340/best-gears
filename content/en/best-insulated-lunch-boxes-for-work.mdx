## What we look for in a work lunch box

- **Real temperature retention**: 6+ hours cold/hot (not the 4 marketing hours)
- **Durability**: survives drops, dust, and the packed locker
- **Right capacity**: container + snacks + thermos, without becoming luggage
- **Easy to clean**: after a chili spill, no smell forever

## Tips to keep food hot longer (without spending more)

1. **Pre-heat the thermos**: boiling water inside for 5 min before adding food — gains 2-3 extra hours
2. **Fill it to the top**: air inside cools faster
3. **Food straight off the stove**: pack it at max temperature, not "still warmish"
4. **Ice first, food after**: freeze gel packs flat the night before
5. **The electric trick**: plug it in 25-30 min before your break, not 5

## FAQ

**How many hours does a good insulated lunch box last?**
Quality ones hold 6-8 hours with pre-heating. Bad ones: 3-4. The electric one doesn't worry: it heats whenever you want.

**Soft bag or hard container?**
Soft bags protect containers better and carry more. Hard ones survive weight stacked on top in the locker.

**Does the electric one use a lot of power?**
No — around 40W, less than a light bulb. Heating your lunch costs cents.
